## Policy
### 1. Introduction
The project maintainer for this Project will only accept contributions using the Developer's Certificate of Origin 1.1  located at [developercertificate.org](https://developercertificate.org) ("DCO"). The DCO is a legally binding statement asserting that you are the creator of your contribution, or that you otherwise have the authority to distribute the contribution, and that you are intentionally making the contribution available under the license associated with the Project ("License"). Additionally, we require that contributions follow the AI guidelines.

### 2. Important Points
* New contributors should not remove existing ANL copyright headers; however, you may add your own name and company to new files you create.

* Pseudonymous or anonymous contributions are permissible, but you must be reachable at the email address provided in the Signed-off-by line.

* If your contribution is significant, you are also welcome to add your name and copyright date to the source file header.

* U.S. Federal law prevents the government from accepting gratuitous services unless certain conditions are met. By submitting a pull request, you acknowledge that your services are offered without expectation of payment and that you expressly waive any future pay claims against the U.S. Federal government related to your contribution.

* If you are a U.S. Federal government employee and use a `*.mil` or `*.gov` email address, we interpret your Signed-off-by to mean that the contribution was created in whole or in part by you and that your contribution is not subject to copyright protections.


### 3. AI Usage

> [!IMPORTANT]
>
> AI-assisted and AI-generated contributions are permitted, but all AI involvement must be fully discolsed. You are solely responsible for any code you submit.
>
> Undisclosed AI use may result in a permament contribution ban.

Use of GenAI tools when contributing is allowed, provided you meet the following requirements so maintainers can properly evaluate your contributions:

1. Disclose all AI use in the contribution, including AI-assisted, AI-generated, and any other AI involvement.
2. Before submitting AI-assisted or AI-generated code, you must verify it and understand its logic, implementation, and design. You must be prepared to explain these decisions in the pull request and in response to maintainer questions.
3. Clearly document how and to what extent AI was used, including the name of each tool and model, such as ChatGPT, Codex, or Claude.
4.  You may not use AI to draft, rewrite, or generate bug reports, feature requests, pull request descriptions, GitHub Discussions, review comments, or responses to maintainers.

If you are unsure how these requirements apply to your contribution, open a GitHub Discussion or contact a maintainer for clarification.

### 4. Developer Certificate of Origin Process

Before submitting contributing code to this repository you'll need to sign a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) (see below). At a high level, adding your information to this file tells us that you have the right to submit the work you're contributing and indicates that you consent to our treating the contribution in a way consistent with the license associated with this software (as described in [LICENSE](https://github.com/sequence-toolbox/SeQUeNCe/blob/master/LICENSE)) and its documentation ("Project"). Remediation commits are permitted.

To agree to the DCO, each commit must be signed off with a message like:
```Signed-off-by: John Doe <john.doe@university.edu>```. This can be done automatically using `git commit -s`.  Additionally, the author name as configured in git must match the signed-off name. A valid signed off commit will look like

```
Author: John Doe <john.doe@university.edu>
Date:   Sat Feb 29 00:00:00 2026 -0000
    Update file
    Signed-off-by: John Doe <john.doe@university.edu>
```

    this project or the open source license(s) involved.
